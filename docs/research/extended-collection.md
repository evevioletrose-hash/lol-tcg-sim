## FIFA Qatar 2022: 12-card crossover
MGA's [586357/586364 instruction sheet](https://www.mgae.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/586364EUC_MANUAL_EN.pdf), visually inspected p.1, displays football-themed cards with game-stat icons and explicitly directs owners to Dance Off instructions. Printed in 2022. [TCDB checklist](https://www.tcdb.com/Checklist.cfm/sid/353567/2022-LOL-Surprise-X-FIFA-World-Cup-Qatar) and [Alan Jenkins, 16 December 2022](https://cartophilic-info-exch.blogspot.com/2022/12/lol-surprise-lol-surprise-x-fifa-world.html) identify twelve cards included with dolls. This is stronger than a generic licensed collectible lead. See [separate factual checklist](evidence/fifa-2022-checklist.json). Both catalogues agree on the numbered identities. TCDB reports 87.5% image coverage (front/back aggregate, not necessarily 10.5 distinct cards); individual card pages could not be fetched in this session. Exact printed IDs, mechanical stats and effects, and permission/rules for mixing with the 2021 set remain unknown. Do not add them to playable base slots or silently assign 1-401 onward.

## FIFA Women's World Cup 2023: further lead
[Product report](https://www.youloveit.com/dolls/2936-lol-surprise-fifa-womens-world-cup-dolls-2023.html) describes six dolls (Australia, New Zealand, USA, fan, referee, player), each with a card. [Nine, August 13 2023](https://www.nine.com.au/shopping/gift-ideas/fifa-womens-world-cup-merch-list-20230813-p5rmwa.html) corroborates one exclusive card per doll. Retail copy describes card as a photo background. No Dance Off rules reference or card stats verified. Treat as six potential collectible identities, not a confirmed playable expansion. Search original 2023 leaflet and unboxing stills.

## Base set missing identities
Original 400-row TSV still present in restoration at cdaf7d6. It has 89 rows lacking a recognized type or name. 64 have a same-number historical digital record; the remaining 25 have names in the physical collector checklist (page 3). [Gap review dataset](evidence/tabletop-gap-leads.json) records all 89 candidate names, their distinct source kinds, and explicit unverified printed fields. 1-055 and 1-244 already have names but lack typed definitions. This is a research queue, not a replacement for scans or a ready-to-import spreadsheet. No printed stats, rank, phase or effect inferred from name or digital ID. Prioritize the 25 absent from digital configuration for physical acquisition/scans; 1-175 and 1-337 have original digital art as well as configuration candidates.

Additional photographic lead: [January 10 2022 subscription box review](https://hellosubscription.com/lol-surprise-box-winter-2021-review-rising-stars/) includes an actual pack and photographed cards; inspect original-resolution photos privately before transcribing. [eBay singles listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/226348826602) may provide additional card images and variant evidence; ephemeral, no purchase or scan verification performed. Preserve card fronts and backs with any unique QR codes redacted before sharing.
