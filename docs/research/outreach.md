## Interview topics
- Who authored rules, card effects, rank system and balance? What was the studio structure?
- Were rank tiers or club packages assigned mechanically? Which archetypes were intentional?
- What was the expected audience, playtest procedure, organized-play plan and expansion roadmap?
- How did simultaneous effects, phase skipping, priority, hidden cards and venue overrides resolve? Was there an internal FAQ or comprehensive rules document?
- Did digital gameplay use the tabletop rules? Was it native, web or hybrid? What public documentation or assets survive?
- What was announced versus shipped regarding QR redemption, BB Bling, NFTs, prices, age restrictions and safeguards? How was the service wound down?
- Are there permitted card images, card lists, early design documents or colleagues willing to share recollections?
